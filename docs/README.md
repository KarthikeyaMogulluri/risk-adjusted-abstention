These are the working notes kept while the study ran, in order. They are
included because the provenance is the point: the hypothesis was
pre-registered, then refuted by its own authors, and the notes record
each reversal at the moment it happened rather than after the fact.

Two entries are worth reading even if you skip the rest:

- **[The result reverses](2026-09-21_rq1-refuted-and-why-that-is-the-result.md)** — a parser bug and a degenerate cost model turned a favourable result into a refutation.
